<p>The School Management Committee (SMC) oversees the overall management
and strategic direction of the school. Working closely with the Ministry
of Education (MOE), the committee ensures effective school operations,
supports quality teaching and learning, promotes student development and
well-being, manages staff and resources, and strengthens partnerships with
parents and the community to achieve the school's educational goals.</p>
