<p>Welcome to Whitley Secondary School!
<br>Thank you for visiting our website.</p>
<p>It’s been a privilege serving as the 14th Principal of Whitley since its
establishment in 1965, building on the strong foundation put in place by
those who came before us.</p>
<p>At Whitley, we believe that every young person has a light within them
waiting to be discovered, nurtured and shared. Our mission is not simply
to prepare students for examinations, but to help them flourish — as Exemplary
Leaders with the Heart to Care and the Will to Learn.</p>
<p>As we celebrated our 61st Anniversary this year with the theme “Starry
Night”, we reflected on a simple yet profound truth. A single star may
shine brightly, but it is the constellation of many stars that captures
our imagination and guides generations. Likewise, while every Whitleyan
brings unique gifts and aspirations, it is through our shared values, collective
purpose and care for one another that we become something far greater than
ourselves.</p>
<p>The anniversary was also a time of gratitude. We honour the generations
of students, teachers, parents, alumni and partners whose dedication has
shaped Whitley into the caring and vibrant community it is today. Their
legacy reminds us that every act of kindness, every lesson taught and every
life touched leaves a lasting imprint on those who come after us. As beneficiaries
of their efforts, we are inspired to pay it forward and leave the school
stronger for future generations.</p>
<p>Our vision, A Caring and Learning Community of Leaders, reminds us that
leadership is not defined by position or prestige. It is reflected in our
everyday choices—to Persevere in the face of challenges, to show Respect,
to act with Integrity, to exercise Discipline, and to lead with Empathy.
These are the school PRIDE values that shape our character and illuminate
the path for others.</p>
<p>We believe that leadership begins with agency—the conviction that each
of us has the ability and responsibility to make a positive difference.
Here at Whitley, we intentionally create authentic opportunities for students
to lead, make decisions, solve problems and serve others. Through these
experiences, they learn that leadership is not about waiting for permission
to act, but about stepping forward with courage, initiative and a heart
to contribute.</p>
<p>In a world that often encourages us to look down at our devices, Whitley
encourages our students to look up—to dream boldly, remain curious, and
approach life with humility. Like the Artemis II astronauts who looked
back at Earth from space in April 2026 and spoke of its beauty and fragility,
we hope our students will see beyond themselves, appreciate the interconnectedness
of humanity, and recognise that every action, however small, has the power
to make a difference.</p>
<p>We also believe that flourishing is a lifelong journey of growth. It does
not happen by chance, but through daily habits, meaningful relationships,
purposeful reflection and a willingness to embrace challenges. Every lesson,
every setback, every act of service and every opportunity to lead helps
our students become just a little better than yesterday. In celebrating
progress rather than perfection, we nurture resilient learners who view
challenges as opportunities to grow and who continually strive to become
the best version of themselves.Our dedicated staff, supportive parents,
alumni and partners each play a vital part in this journey. Together, we
create a community where every student is known, valued and encouraged
to discover their own light while helping others find theirs.</p>
<p>As you explore our school, I hope you will see more than our programmes
and achievements. I hope you will experience the warmth of our community,
the strength of our shared purpose, and our unwavering belief that every
young person can flourish.</p>
<p>May every Whitleyan be grateful for those who have gone before us, courageous
in shaping the future before us, and committed to growing into leaders
who shine not for themselves alone, but for others. For when each of us
discovers our own light and chooses to shine for others, together we become
a constellation that lights the way for generations to come.</p>
<p>Yours sincerely,
<br>Lynn Koh
<br>Principal</p>
